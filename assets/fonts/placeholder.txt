Place NotoSans font files here:
  - NotoSans-Regular.ttf
  - NotoSans-Bold.ttf
  - NotoSans-SemiBold.ttf

Download from: https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans

Alternatively, replace the font references in pubspec.yaml and app_theme.dart
with the google_fonts package to load fonts at runtime without bundling them.
